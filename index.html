Separate signals, same watchlist. These are descriptive indicators, not buy/sell guarantees.
3 models enabled

01 · ROTY score

Primary screen. Uses card price and the positive American odds profit multiplier, per your specification.

score = card_price × odds / 100

Green < $100 · Yellow < $120 · Orange < $150 · Red ≥ $150

02 · Odds movement

Compares the latest odds to the previous odds value you recorded. Positive movement means the listed number increased (longer odds); negative means it shortened.

movement = current odds − prior odds

Historical snapshots are needed for reliable automated trend tracking.

03 · Card-price momentum

Compares current price with your previous price entry. This prototype uses the previous price as a baseline.

momentum % = (current − prior) / prior × 100

Use comparable completed sales for the same parallel and grade.

Next: connect automated feeds

Designed for free hosting with scheduled data refreshes.
  1. Choose an odds provider that exposes NFL OROY futures and confirm its free-tier limits.
  2. Choose a reliable card price/sold-comps provider for the exact red-logo parallel. Marketplace asking prices alone are not sold values.
  3. Move data and API keys to a small backend (never put secret keys in browser code).
  4. Schedule a backend job every 6 hours to save snapshots, recalculate models, and flag changes.
  5. Deploy this frontend on GitHub Pages or another static host; use a free backend scheduler/database tier if available.
Rookie Edge is an analytics prototype. Odds are market prices, not guaranteed probabilities. Card prices may be illiquid and feeds may be delayed. Verify Fanatics eligibility and redemption terms separately. This prototype does not place bets or trades.
